# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give actionable feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove content and may restrict participation for conduct they consider inappropriate. Report concerns privately through the contact method on the repository owner's GitHub profile.
